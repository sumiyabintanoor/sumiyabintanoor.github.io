Research

Prevalence, Phenotypic Resistance Patterns, and Genotypic Characterization of Multidrug-Resistant ESBL-Producing Escherichia coli from Broiler Chickens in Live Bird Markets of Feni Sadar Upazila, Bangladesh

In my research journey, I performed sample collection from live bird markets, isolation of E. coli on selective media, biochemical identification, antimicrobial susceptibility testing, phenotypic ESBL confirmation, biofilm formation assay and molecular analysis of resistance genes.

Training Programme

November 2023 · 3 weeks

Laboratory Techniques in Infectious Diseases

Child Health Research Foundation (CHRF) · Building Scientists for BangladeshCHRF Headquarter, Dhaka and CHRF Laboratory, Bangladesh Shishu Hospital & Institute (BSHI)

A hands-on programme covering pathogen identification, clinical sample collection, biosafety, molecular diagnostics, antimicrobial susceptibility testing, data analysis and bio-banking, including in-patient and out-patient visits at the country’s largest paediatric hospital.

Week 1 Molecular techniques Nucleic acid extraction and PCR for the detection and characterisation of pathogens.
Week 2 Clinical microbiology Aseptic collection and handling of clinical samples such as blood and CSF at BSHI, followed by culture, isolation, identification and antimicrobial susceptibility testing.
Week 3 Phage detection Introduced to CHRF’s typhoid surveillance, which detects Salmonella Typhi-specific bacteriophages in environmental water.
